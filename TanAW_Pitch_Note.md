# TanAW — Pitch Note (vs. Google Maps)

## One-line pitch
TanAW is an **accessibility-first** navigation and community platform for Mindanao — it doesn't just show routes, it shows **step-free, barrier-aware routes** verified by the community, plus AI storefront auditing.

## The problem Google Maps doesn't solve
Google Maps optimizes for speed and traffic. It is **not** built for wheelchair users, parents with strollers, the elderly, or anyone with a mobility challenge:
- It rarely flags steps, ramps, door widths, or threshold obstacles.
- It has no community verification of accessibility claims.
- It offers no storefront-level accessibility audit.
- Its data is generic and centrally controlled, not locally crowdsourced.

## What makes TanAW unique

| # | Unique capability | Why it beats Google Maps for accessibility |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Step-free route planning** with live OSRM accessible route preview | Routes are filtered for barriers, not just shortest path |
| 2 | **40+ granular accessibility tags** (zero-step entry, tactile paving, auto door openers, wide ADA restrooms, elevator status) | Far more detail than Google's generic "wheelchair accessible" flag |
| 3 | **AI storefront auditing** via computer vision (Gemini) | Snap a photo of an entrance → instant ramp grade, door width, step count, threshold obstacle report |
| 4 | **Crowdsourced, community-verified pins** with contribution history & leaderboard | Locally accountable data, not black-box central data |
| 5 | **Mindanao city viewport switcher** | Hyper-local focus (Cagayan de Oro pilot) instead of one-size-fits-all global data |
| 6 | **Desktop GIS analytical suite** | Municipal planners can review audits, filter barrier reports, export GeoJSON |
| 7 | **On-the-go mobile UI** with turn-by-turn step-free routing & quick photo-pin submissions | Built for the people who actually need accessible navigation |
| 8 | **WCAG AAA contrast** neo-brutalist high-contrast design | Designed for low vision from day one |
| 9 | **Open source, zero-cost, privacy-by-design** | No lock-in, no tracking, community-owned |
| 10 | **In-app community messaging** | Connects users, volunteers, NGOs, and city planners |
