Our commitment

AccessMap pairs crowdsourced pins with AI vision to build a trustworthy accessibility atlas. We apply the same standard to our own product: every screen, map control and photo-scan result should work for people using a keyboard, screen reader, voice control or touch.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA across the web app and review it each release.
